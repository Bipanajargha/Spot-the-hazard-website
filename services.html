What we can do for you

The skills behind Spot the Hazard, and what we can bring to your site.

3D browser development

Walkable 3D warehouse levels that run in a browser tab on the laptops you already own. No install, no headset.

Scoring and hazard logic

Spotting and responding are both scored, with a star rating and a breakdown of hazards found, responses, wrong clicks, hints and time.

UI/UX design

A clear HUD, plain-language prompts and a short session, designed for people with limited technical confidence.

Safety content

Eight hazards written from real induction checklists, each with a right and wrong response, placed differently in every layout.

Playtesting and QA

Every level is played through to confirm each hazard is findable and fairly scored before a learner sees it.

Client-led iteration

Built with client feedback at each stage, which added the hint feature, wrong-click penalty and a wider gap between levels.

Who it is for

Small and mid-sized warehousing and logistics operators who need inductions that prove competence, without VR hardware or an IT project.

Induction leads

Add a practical, scored step to the induction you already run, instead of another slide or signature.

Safety and compliance officers

Keep a score breakdown per person as evidence that hazard awareness was tested, not just attended.

Site and shift managers

Run a quick 80-second level at the start of an intake using the laptops and browsers you already have.
