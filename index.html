Our mission

Make noticing a habit, so every shift starts safer than the last.

How a session runs

The same four beats every time.

01

Start in the safe room

Same warehouse, no hazards. Learn the controls first.

02

Walk the aisles

WASD, drag to look, or click the floor to walk.

03

Click what's wrong

Any hazard, any order. Wrong clicks cost points.

04

Say what you'd do

Report it, fix it, or ignore it. This is scored too.

Why it holds attention when the slide deck doesn't

Three things induction videos structurally cannot do.

A different warehouse every level

One aisle for orientation, two parallel aisles at midday rush, a loading dock, then a three-aisle full shift audit. Nobody memorises a route.

Spot it, then fix it

Every hazard asks for the correct real-world response. "Ignore it" is always on the list, and choosing it is always wrong.

A score you can put in a file

Each run returns a star rating and a full breakdown: hazards found, responses, wrong guesses, hints and time bonus. Evidence of competence, not attendance.
