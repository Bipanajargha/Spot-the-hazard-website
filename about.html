About Safe360 Training Systems · Team Bliss

We built the warehouse we kept being warned about.

Hazard-perception training that puts a person inside the space, not in front of a slide.

Why we started

Warehouse induction is mostly reading: a folder, a video, a signature. It doesn't prepare anyone for the cable across the walkway.

The gap isn't knowledge. It's the habit of noticing what's wrong, and the confidence to act on it.

So we built a walkable warehouse with the eight hazards from real checklists, scored the response as much as the spot, and put it in a browser tab.

Mission: make noticing a habit, so every shift starts safer than the last.
Noticing is a skill. Skills need reps.

How we got here

24 AugKick-off with our client, Sulav Graj - problem, eight hazards and a 'spot it, then fix it' approach agreed
3 SepRequirements approved - hint feature added at the client's request
10 SepPrototype review - dark and gold theme approved, wrong-click penalty added
PresentationClient rated us 5 out of 5 on all five presentation criteria
NextClient feedback to act on - clearer on-screen update once a hazard is handled, and a bigger difficulty gap between levels. Then site packs and a supervisor dashboard.
