Before you start

Any modern browser on a laptop or desktop. A mouse helps but trackpad and touch both work. No headset, no plugin, no sign-up.

A level takes 80 seconds. Add a minute for the safe room if it's your first run.

What's in every warehouse

Racking bays with painted lane lines, a forklift and hi-vis workers, a fire exit with sign and extinguisher, 5-8 live hazards in 2-3 phases.

Hazards have no individual timer.

Click them in any order, the moment you see them.
